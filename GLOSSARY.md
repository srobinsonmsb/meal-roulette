# Meal Roulette

Meal Roulette helps a household document meals, plan its weekly meals, and build a grocery list, with an initial focus on dinner.

## Language

**Household**:
A group of people who share and manage a weekly meal plan.

**Meal**:
A reusable choice of what to eat, such as chicken tacos or salmon with rice and broccoli. A meal does not require a recipe.

**Ingredient**:
A food item used to make a meal.

**Serving**:
The amount of a meal intended for one person.

**Meal yield**:
The number of servings produced by a meal's recorded ingredient amounts.

**Effort**:
The household's estimate of the work involved in preparing a meal: quick, moderate, or involved.

**Recipe**:
Instructions for preparing a meal, including its ingredients.

**Grocery list**:
A household's list of ingredients to buy for its planned meals.

**Routine**:
A reusable weekly pattern for the household's meal planning, such as a quick-dinner night or a regular evening eating out. A particular week's plan may make exceptions to the routine.

**Weekly exception**:
A change to a routine for a particular date and mealtime in one weekly meal plan.

**Weekly meal plan**:
A single household's intended meals for a week.
_Avoid_: Weekly menu, weekly schedule

**Draft plan**:
A weekly meal plan still being prepared before household confirmation.

**Confirmed plan**:
A weekly meal plan the household has accepted as its intended schedule.

**Meal assignment**:
A person's planned meal for a particular date and mealtime.

**Group meal**:
A meal planned jointly for multiple household members.

**Solo meal**:
A meal planned independently for one household member.

**Preference list**:
A person's set of meals approved for automatic selection.

**Leftovers**:
A dinner label indicating that no meal is planned because leftovers will be eaten.

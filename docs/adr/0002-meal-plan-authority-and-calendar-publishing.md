# Meal plan authority and calendar publishing

Meal Roulette owns the household's confirmed meal plan, and publishing to external calendars or planners is one-way. Changes made in Meal Roulette update the corresponding published events; edits made directly in an external calendar do not change the household plan. Google Calendar is the initial integration, with other destinations deferred, keeping the plan and its derived grocery list under household control without bidirectional synchronization.
